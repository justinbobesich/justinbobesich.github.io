Engineering|Design|Building

I’ve been taking things from an idea to a working build since I was young, from web shooters and 3D-printed products to a working arcade claw machine, a suction wall climber and a robotic arm that recognises faces and responds to voice commands. I enjoy figuring out how mechanisms work, testing my ideas and improving them when the first version fails.

I also started a 3D printing business, taking requests from customers and making products for them. It taught me to turn an idea into something someone else wanted and could use.

I’m in Grade 12 and plan to study engineering starting in Fall 2027. This site documents some of the things I’ve made, including the prototypes and the parts that didn’t work the first time.

How I work

Idea → Design → Prototype → Test → Improve

This is the pattern most of my projects follow. Hardly any of them work on the first try, so most go around the loop more than once.

Idea

Usually something I want to exist, or a question like “could I actually make that work?”

Design

Working out how it could function and what parts it needs before I start building.

Prototype

A first version, built to find out what I got wrong.

Test

Finding where it fails: what slips, jams, breaks or doesn’t hold.

Improve

Change what failed, then test again. Repeat until it works reliably.

↻ Test and Improve repeat until the design does what it needs to.
